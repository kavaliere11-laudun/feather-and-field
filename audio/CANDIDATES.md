The sources:
- BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin, France). Licence CC0: free for a paid game, no credit needed. Each sound page has a play
  button and Download buttons; the MP3 file itself is at `https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/NNNN.mp3`, where NNNN is the
  page's number.
- US National Park Service sound gallery. Public domain; the gallery asks for a credit line ("National Park Service").
  The park service is used only where BigSoundBank has nothing: geese and bees.
