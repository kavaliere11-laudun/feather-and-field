Feather & Field: the sounds and music

Music (Audrey's picks, 28 Oct 2026). Both CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): free for a paid game, no credit required.
Credited anyway, with thanks.
- "Champ de tournesol" by Komiku, from the album "It's time for adventure!". Free Music Archive and the Internet Archive both
  list it as CC0 1.0 Universal. The copy used here came from FreePD.com.
- "Happy Whistling Ukulele" by Rafael Krux, from FreePD.com ("CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication", "Free for Commercial
  Use").
Both were made quieter and evened out to the same loudness (-18 LUFS), and saved as 128 kbps MP3s.

The hens chatting (29 Oct 2026): Audrey's own hens, from her own videos. Every voice was taken out; seven of their real
moments were kept whole and in order, gently cleaned (some of the garden's air left in). audio/hens/hen1-7.mp3.

The farm's sounds (29 Oct 2026; Audrey: "All good, put them in"), audio/farm/: real recordings by Joseph Sardin,
BigSoundBank.com, CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain; credit optional, given with thanks: "Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN -
BigSoundBank.com"). Cut, made gentler where needed and evened out by the source's audio/farm_clips.py:
- the egg songs: "Hen lays #1"; the rooster: "Cock Song #1", "Rooster Song"; the fox's visit: "Henhouse";
- a mummy hen with her chicks: "Hens worried"; chicks: "Chick that chirp"; ducks: "Ducks"; Biscuit: "Old dog barking #1";
- the pond: "Pond in a Field"; dawn: "Birds Waking #1"; day: "Countryside #3"; evening: "Evening Birds"; night:
  "Campaign at night #1".

The geese and the bee (29 Oct 2026), audio/farm/honk1-3.mp3, buzz1-2.mp3: the US National Park Service's sound gallery
("Canada Geese", "Bee", Yosemite), public domain ("The files are in the public domain and may be downloaded. Enjoy, and
